National Infrastructure Development Act
Publication Date: 2026-06-01
Ministry: Ministry of Transport and Infrastructure

Short Policy Description:
The National Infrastructure Development Act sets out a framework for upgrading national road and rail networks to improve connectivity, safety, and support economic growth. The policy prioritizes public-private partnerships and mandates environmental and social impact assessments for major projects.

Affected Companies:
- TransGlobal Constructions Ltd.
- AeroRail Systems Inc.

Budget Allocation:
The act allocates a provisional budget of USD 250000000 (two hundred fifty million US dollars) for the first phase of projects.

Implementation Timeline:
The implementation is planned over a 5-year horizon starting from 2026-07-01 with phased milestones each fiscal year.

Key Provisions:
1. Establishment of a national infrastructure steering committee.
2. Requirement for competitive tendering for major contracts.
3. Support for local content and workforce training.
